About Aiden

Aiden Cole is a documentary photographer based in Portland, Oregon, with a decade of experience shooting for independent cafés, roasteries, and coffee brands. His work has appeared in regional food publications and on packaging for several small-batch roasters.

Aiden's approach favors natural light, unposed moments, and close attention to texture — steam rising off a cup, the grain of a wooden counter, hands wrapped around a warm mug on a cold morning.

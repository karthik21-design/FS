About the College

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) was established in the academic year 2001–02 and is permanently affiliated to Andhra University, Visakhapatnam. Approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and accredited by NAAC with an 'A' grade, the institute offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and technology, and has held autonomous status since 2015.

ANITS students frequently publish research in areas such as Water (H2O) treatment and environmental engineering, with several papers ranked 1st at national-level conferences.

Why Choose ANITS?

Our Key Strengths (Unordered List)

  • Permanently affiliated to Andhra University and NAAC 'A' grade accredited
  • Autonomous institution with modern laboratories and Wi-Fi enabled campus
  • Strong industry ties under Megha Engineering and Infrastructure Limited (MEIL)
  • Vibrant 12-acre campus in Sangivalasa, close to the Chennai–Kolkata Highway

Admission Process (Ordered List)

  1. Appear for AP EAMCET (for B.Tech admissions)
  2. Register through the EAMCET web counselling portal
  3. Exercise seat options based on rank (Category-A) or apply under Management/NRI quota (Category-B)
  4. Report to ANITS and confirm admission by fee payment
